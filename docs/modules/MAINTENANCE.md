## 11 October 2026 — task-first work register

Home now shows accurate company-wide open/urgent/overdue/down/service-due counts,
with an oldest-due work queue and a linked service-due equipment shortlist. Search,
work type, status, assigned-to-me and 50-record paging reach historical work. Fleet
identities and vehicle choices require the independent Fleet read capability and
app availability. The work form has one required equipment-or-vehicle selector;
its hidden fields always submit exactly one asset. For assets outside the bounded
quick picker, search the equipment register and create work from the asset record.
Guides covers fault reporting, technician work, parts/evidence, downtime and closure.
Existing maintenance/service/stock/Finance integration boundaries remain unchanged.
