# HR workspace research — 8 October 2026

Official product references reviewed to identify ordinary platform workflows:

- [Personio Core HR](https://www.personio.com/product/core-hr-software/): shared employee
  records across the lifecycle, performance/development and reporting. Atlas response:
  operational HR home and source-linked employee/lifecycle reports.
- [Personio absence management](https://www.personio.com/product/absence-management/):
  employee balances, manager decisions and calendar visibility. Atlas preserves the
  existing working-day holiday, absence and Scheduling workflows rather than copying them.
- [BambooHR platform overview](https://www.bamboohr.com/demo): hiring/onboarding,
  payroll/time and employee experience/performance as connected parts. Atlas response:
  internal vacancies/applicants with explicit accepted-offer handover to canonical Employee.
- [BambooHR employee self service](https://www.bamboohr.com/blog/self-onboarding): employees
  handle their own information and requests. Atlas response: retain My HR and add own
  learning, without opening company employee records or confidential recruitment.

These are product comparisons, not claims that Atlas duplicates their whole platform.
